Best Student Summit Project

Recognized by Professor Seki and the Vietnam–Japan Youth Exchange Organizing Committee

Among all participating teams, our project on Free Child Organization was selected as the most outstanding presentation for its innovation, cross-cultural collaboration, and contribution to sustainable development discussions addressing child labor through SDG frameworks.

🎯 SDG 1 · No Poverty 📚 SDG 4 · Quality Education ⚖️ SDG 8 · Decent Work 🤝 SDG 17 · Partnerships
The Moment of Truth

Six Students. One Presentation.
One Prize.

Weeks of research, late-night discussions, and cross-cultural collaboration led to this stage. When the FCO poster was raised and the microphone switched on, every preparation found its purpose.

🔬

The Problem

Child labor remains one of humanity's most persistent injustices — a cycle our team chose to interrupt through the lens of the Free Child Organization model.

🌐

The Solution

We proposed a partnership model connecting Save the Children, ILO, World Vision, and IIECL — a sustainable funding and intervention framework born from two countries thinking as one.

🏅

The Recognition

Professor Seki and the organizing committee awarded our team Best Student Summit Project — validating months of work and the power of international collaboration in solving global challenges.
